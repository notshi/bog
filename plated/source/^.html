#^email trim=ends Email #^bandcamp trim=ends Bandcamp #^instagram trim=ends Instagram #^about_text trim=ends form=markdown shi Blank was one of ten artists commissioned by Bradford Culture Company to explore specific sites on the Bradford Pennine Gateway National Nature Reserve. They have been exploring Bingley North Bog and its wildlife over the past three months. As part of their residency, shi will come together with their avantgarde ambient duo, Gilank for an improvised performance at dusk using field recordings at the bog. A Duet with Bingley Bog seeks to question the impact of human activities on our nature reserves and what this relationship could be if we stop polluting our environment. This Bog is 10,000 years old and plays a huge role in our existence on the planet. It is right next to the A650 motorway and has only been granted official National Nature Reserve status very recently.
