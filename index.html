residency

24.10.2026

I feel really fortunate to have been selected for this residency. 3 months really flew by and my improvised performance will happen this Sunday, rain or storm. Sometimes you can get bogged (lol) down with the details of the work but I promised a friend that I would slow down and appreciate the experience in its entirety ~ whenever I can. Hopefully, this deliberate contemplation of my relationship with mother nature has translated across to you through this sharing, be it the field recordings, the zine, the score or the performance.

I'm really looking forward to the improvised performance with Alice and I hope you'll join us for an hour of ambient, strange and hopefully, calming soundscape that is entirely inspired by my visits to the bog.

About

shi Blank was one of ten artists commissioned by Bradford Culture Company to explore specific sites on the Bradford Pennine Gateway National Nature Reserve.

They have been exploring Bingley North Bog and its wildlife over the past three months. As part of their residency, shi will come together with their avantgarde ambient duo, Gilank for an improvised performance at dusk using field recordings at the bog. A Duet with Bingley Bog seeks to question the impact of human activities on our nature reserves and what this relationship could be if we stop polluting our environment.

This Bog is 10,000 years old and plays a huge role in our existence on the planet. It is right next to the A650 motorway and has only been granted official National Nature Reserve status very recently.
